# Improve

Use when the owner requests building software or carrying improvements through
implementation and verification. Compose the existing cards; the [core](../../SKILL.md)
continues to control evidence and authority. Audit-only work uses Investigate.

## Procedure

1. Record engagement mode, targets, effects and acceptance in the existing map
   or work record as the [core](../../SKILL.md#authority) requires. Reuse current
   authorization; ask only for a decision that blocks a concrete effect.
2. Use [Understand](understand.md) for the relevant baseline and product contract.
   For new software, establish one working core journey before expanding features.
   For partial software, separate unfinished promises from regressions. For a
   claimed finished product, exercise its claims against actual behavior.
3. Apply [quality tests](../quality-tests.md) and [product principles](../product-principles.md)
   to relevant journeys. Use [Investigate](investigate.md) to reproduce consequential
   gaps, inspect counterevidence and preserve strengths. Separate defects,
   justified improvements, hypotheses and preferences.
4. Compare plausible alternatives for consequential changes under the
   [quality tests](../quality-tests.md). Prefer the smallest coherent solution;
   explain when a broader repair removes the cause. Do not invent features or
   rewrite sound work to fill a checklist.
5. Use [Remediate](remediate.md) for supported authorized changes. Continue until
   the requested outcome is concrete and reviewable, including tests and recovery.
   Carry forward approvals within their targets and effects; present a concrete
   decision packet before a newly consequential or unapproved operation.
6. Use [Verify](verify.md) for the affected technical and user outcomes. Confirm
   expected work, not just exit status. Revisit every acceptance criterion and
   deferred obligation after the final change. New evidence can reopen a finding;
   preference alone cannot justify another rewrite.

## Completion and interruption

Finish when requested acceptance obligations are satisfied or a named external
decision blocks them. Continue independent authorized work while blocked. Preserve
an identity-bound checkpoint before long work: evidence, changes, permissions,
open obligations and next check. Revalidate changed dependencies on resume.

Report what changed and why, what was preserved, executed checks and their scope,
unresolved hypotheses, blocked effects and the smallest next action. Do not turn
agent simulation into participant research, a plan into execution, or a prepared
release into a deployed product. Publication and installation need their own
authorization; they are not implied by a green local test.
